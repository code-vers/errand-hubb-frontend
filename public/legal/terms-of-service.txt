PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY: HOW ERRANDHUBB
WORKS
This summary is provided to help Users understand the marketplace. If this summary conflicts with the

FOR CLIENTS
1. Create a Client account.
Clients can use ErrandHubb to find people who perform errands and local services.
2. Search for an Errander or post an Errand.
A Client can browse available Erranders or describe a Job that needs to be completed.
3. Review the Errander.
The Client can review available profile information, pricing, services, experience, reviews, and other
information.
4. Communicate before hiring.
Client and Errander discuss the Job, price, time, location, expenses, and other details.
5. Choose your Errander.
ErrandHubb does not ordinarily choose the worker for the Client. The Client decides whom to hire.
6. Agree on the Job and price.
22
The Client and Errander reach their own agreement.
7. Errander performs the Job.
The Errander independently performs the agreed service.
8. Client pays the Errander.
Unless ErrandHubb expressly introduces a payment feature, the Client pays the Errander directly according
to their agreement.
FOR ERRANDERS
1. Create an Errander profile.
Describe the services you offer and provide accurate profile information.
2. Choose your membership.
Standard pricing is currently:
$5 per month
or
$50 per year paid upfront
3. Set your own service pricing.
Erranders establish or negotiate their own prices.
4. Search for opportunities and receive inquiries.
Clients may contact Erranders directly, and Erranders may be able to respond to Jobs posted on the
Platform.
5. Communicate with the Client.
Agree upon price, timing, location, expenses, and scope of work.
6. Decide whether to accept the Job.
Erranders are not required by ErrandHubb to accept every available Job.

8. Receive payment from the Client.
Under the standard marketplace model, ErrandHubb does not take a percentage commission from the
